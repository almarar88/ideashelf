# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release of Guilty.

- Pass-and-play mystery game for 3–10 players, fully in Arabic and RTL.
- Four roles: Detective, Silent Witness, Culprit, Innocent Suspect.
- Hold-to-reveal fingerprint hand-out; nothing is shown on a tap alone.
- Case briefing with three clues gated behind a second fingerprint, for the
  Silent Witness only.
- 2 / 3 / 5-minute interrogation timer with an ambient ticking loop, a
  three-question budget for the Silent Witness, and 17 silent hint cards.
- "AI defence lawyer" button that hands the Culprit a ready-made alibi.
- Verdict screen with a gavel, confetti on a correct accusation, and a
  cross-round score board.
- 54 offline cases across three categories, plus optional Gemini generation
  for brand-new cases and alibis.
